description=[h2]制作与使用[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]原生制作菜单“蘇清的返鲜餐盒”分类，两条路线产出同一种餐盒。
description=[*]改造：原版空餐盒×1＋怀表×1＋电子表×1＋经典腕表×1＋电线×2。
description=[*]新制：小金属板×2＋上述三类钟表各1＋电线×2。
description=[*]两条配方均需螺丝刀，工具保留。用于改造的餐盒须清空、移出双手、取消收藏，且没有升级或制冷记录；三类表须是不同实物。
description=[*]食品直接放入盒内，右键查看状态及开关。有效加载时工作；离区后仅在原食品、原餐盒与启用记录可靠时补算游戏时间。退出游戏的现实时间不计。
description=[/list]

description=[h2]默认规格与沙盒[/h2]
description=容量4、自重1.0、减重50%。独立设置：返鲜开关（开）、新盒首次容量（默认4，1–30）、返鲜耗时（默认10游戏小时，0.25–1000）、允许变质返鲜（开）。每10有效游戏小时恢复一个食品自身的完整新鲜期；不同腐坏程度的完成时间不同。基础容量只设置一次，不覆盖已有盒或强化容量。

description=[*]只处理直接存放、具有标准保鲜期的Food，保留原版单件尺寸限制。烧焦、受精蛋、无标准保鲜期食物不处理；不补份量、不改变生熟、不清除毒素或污染。
description=[*]变质返鲜要求世界“移除腐坏食品”为负值；否则跳过已变质食物，右键提示原因。模组不会替你修改世界设置。

description=[*]停用前取出食品并处理自定义餐盒，正常保存退出。

description=[h1]SuQing's Freshness Lunchbox[/h1]
description=Smells good—dig in! A portable food container that gradually restores freshness. Carry it in either hand or keep it in a bag, furniture, vehicle or loaded ground storage. No batteries or power are needed. Eligible food restores together.
description=
description=[h2]Craft and use[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Use the native crafting category “SuQing's Freshness Lunchbox.” Both recipes make the same container.
description=[*]Convert: 1 empty vanilla lunchbox + 1 pocket watch + 1 digital watch + 1 classic wristwatch + 2 electric wires.
description=[*]Build: 2 small metal sheets + one each of those three watch families + 2 electric wires.
description=[*]Both need a screwdriver, which is kept. The source lunchbox must be empty, out of both hands, unfavorited and free of upgrade/refrigeration records. The three watches must be distinct items.
description=[*]Place food directly inside; right-click for the switch and status. Loaded positions work normally. Unloaded game time is credited only with verifiable original food, box and enabled-session records. Real time outside the game never counts.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Defaults and sandbox settings[/h2]
description=Capacity 4, weight 1.0, weight reduction 50%. Separate settings: restoration enabled; initial capacity 4 (1–30); restoration time 10 game hours (0.25–1000); rotten-food support enabled. Each 10 credited hours restores one of each food's own complete fresh-life intervals; completion time varies with age. Initial capacity applies once and never overwrites existing boxes or upgrades.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility and limits[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]No external mod is required. Ordinary Container Upgrades 1.0.3 onward optionally supports this box, without a separate lunchbox compatibility mod. Empty and unhand it before upgrading. This does not add EX or Auto support.
description=[*]Only directly stored standard perishable Food is processed. The vanilla item-size limit remains. Burnt food, fertilized eggs and unsupported shelf lives are excluded. Portions, cooking state, poison and contamination are unchanged.
description=[*]Already rotten food requires a negative world “Rotten Food Removal” setting; otherwise it is skipped with an explanation. World settings are never changed automatically.
description=[*]For 42.21 single-player; multiplayer restoration is not provided. The rc.5 fallback ground outline is a fixed-direction approximation. Rotation, extended placement and Viewpoint visuals require gameplay confirmation.
description=[*]Remove food and dispose of custom containers before disabling, then save and exit normally.
description=[/list]
description=Held and ground models are independently derived from vanilla lunchbox assets. Cover and small icons are separately created series artwork.
