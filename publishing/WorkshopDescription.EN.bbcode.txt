[h1]SuQing's Freshness Lunchbox[/h1]
Smells good. Dig in! Put eligible food directly inside, then choose Seal Lunchbox and Start Restoration from the context menu. You can carry the sealed box while access stays locked. The batch restores the food and unlocks when due. An overdue batch completes when the box is loaded again.

[h2]Crafting and settings[/h2]
[list]
[*]The native crafting menu offers two routes. Use an empty vanilla lunchbox or two small metal sheets, plus one pocket watch, one digital watch, one classic wristwatch and two electric wires. You keep the screwdriver.
[*]Base capacity defaults to 4 and ranges from 1 to 30. A batch defaults to 12 game hours, with a range of 0.25–24. A separate sandbox option controls rotten food.
[*]Load standard perishable food directly. Remove burnt food, tainted food, fertilized eggs and unsupported items. Finish active batches before disabling the mod or rolling back.
[/list]

[h2]Backpack attachment and optional weight fix[/h2]
Uses existing ordinary, large and ALICE backpack-bottom bedroll slots. Ordinary Container Upgrades 1.0.3 is optional. Detach and empty the box before upgrading or restoring it.
The included Lunchbox Attached Weight Fix applies the current container reduction to food in an attached box. Enable [b]SuQingLunchboxAttachedWeight_4222[/b] alongside the lunchbox and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy 2.3.4[/url], approve the Java mod and restart. The main lunchbox can be enabled on its own.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Lunchbox 0.2.0-rc.4 and weight fix 0.1.0-rc.1. Offline B42.22.2 single-player checks cover sealed batches, Loot Tracker 1.8.5 and native carry weight on the three backpack slots. In-game testing is pending. Held and ground models are independently derived from the vanilla lunchbox.
