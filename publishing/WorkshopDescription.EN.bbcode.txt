[h1]SuQing's Freshness Lunchbox[/h1]
Smells good—dig in! A portable food container that gradually restores freshness. Carry it in either hand or keep it in a bag, furniture, vehicle or loaded ground storage. No batteries or power are needed. Eligible food restores together.

[h2]Craft and use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Use the native crafting category “SuQing's Freshness Lunchbox.” Both recipes make the same container.
[*]Convert: 1 empty vanilla lunchbox + 1 pocket watch + 1 digital watch + 1 classic wristwatch + 2 electric wires.
[*]Build: 2 small metal sheets + one each of those three watch families + 2 electric wires.
[*]Both need a screwdriver, which is kept. The source lunchbox must be empty, out of both hands, unfavorited and free of upgrade/refrigeration records. The three watches must be distinct items.
[*]Place food directly inside; right-click for the switch and status. Loaded positions work normally. Unloaded game time is credited only with verifiable original food, box and enabled-session records. Real time outside the game never counts.
[/list]

[h2]Defaults and sandbox settings[/h2]
Capacity 4, weight 1.0, weight reduction 50%. Separate settings: restoration enabled; initial capacity 4 (1–30); restoration time 10 game hours (0.25–1000); rotten-food support enabled. Each 10 credited hours restores one of each food's own complete fresh-life intervals; completion time varies with age. Initial capacity applies once and never overwrites existing boxes or upgrades.

[h2]Compatibility and limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]No external mod is required. Ordinary Container Upgrades 1.0.3 onward optionally supports this box, without a separate lunchbox compatibility mod. Empty and unhand it before upgrading. This does not add EX or Auto support.
[*]Only directly stored standard perishable Food is processed. The vanilla item-size limit remains. Burnt food, fertilized eggs and unsupported shelf lives are excluded. Portions, cooking state, poison and contamination are unchanged.
[*]Already rotten food requires a negative world “Rotten Food Removal” setting; otherwise it is skipped with an explanation. World settings are never changed automatically.
[*]For 42.21 single-player; multiplayer restoration is not provided. The rc.5 fallback ground outline is a fixed-direction approximation. Rotation, extended placement and Viewpoint visuals require gameplay confirmation.
[*]Remove food and dispose of custom containers before disabling, then save and exit normally.
[/list]
Held and ground models are independently derived from vanilla lunchbox assets. Cover and small icons are separately created series artwork.
